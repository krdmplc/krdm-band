# Music distribution: leaving DistroKid?

Status: written 2026-11-23. **Decide in March 2027**: DistroKid is paid until it
renews on **6 April 2027**, and a move needs a few weeks before that. Prices
below were collected on 2026-11-23 and must be checked again then.

Part of the Music section, see [music-brief.md](music-brief.md).

## Where we are

- **DistroKid costs $89.99 a year.** It renews on 6 April 2027.
- **The music earns about $4 a year through it.** All releases together earned
  $44.68 from 2019-04 to 2026-09, and $4.36 in the last 12 months. Details in
  the music brief, "What the DistroKid data says".
- So we pay about **20 times what comes in**. Spotify has paid nothing since
  April 2024 (it pays only for tracks with 1,000+ streams a year), and that won't
  change by switching distributor.
- The catalogue to move: **14 releases by 5 artists**, 5 of them EPs or albums
  and 9 singles, **44 tracks** in all.
- Distribution is worth keeping for **presence**: the bands should be findable
  on every platform. It is not a source of income. The money that can matter is
  SAZAS and IPF (radio and public play), Bandcamp, merch and gigs.

## Suggestions

1. **Move to a distributor without a yearly fee. RouteNote Free first**, Feiyr
   second if RouteNote fails a must-have. A one-off fee (Feiyr, CD Baby) is
   fine too, but there is no reason to pay a yearly one at this income.
2. **Confirm the must-haves in writing** (support chat or FAQ) before moving:
   - several artists under one account;
   - our existing **ISRCs** accepted, so play counts, playlists and Shazam
     history carry over; UPCs too if possible;
   - releases stay live without any subscription;
   - CSV earnings exports by store, country, track and month;
   - YouTube Official Artist Channels can still be requested;
   - they accept an association or an individual from Slovenia, and how they
     pay out (and that money below the payout minimum doesn't expire).
3. **Cancel the paid DistroKid add-ons now** if they renew separately (the
   Social Media Pack earned $0.014 in a year). Don't touch the main plan until
   the move is done, or the releases come down.
4. **Don't pay for video distribution.** Upload videos to the bands' YouTube
   channels. If one video ever really needs Apple Music or Vevo, buy just that
   one (Amuse, Horus Music).
5. **Don't buy "Leave a Legacy".** The releases move instead.

Until the app's "where it's live" checks exist, check by hand on Spotify,
Apple Music and Deezer. Briefly showing twice on a platform is harmless;
showing nowhere is not, so take down at DistroKid last.
